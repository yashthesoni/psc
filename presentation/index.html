Problem & Solution

  • Users rely on weak passwords or exposed credentials.
  • Checking passwords online risks privacy leaks.
  • Our tool checks passwords locally and uses k-anonymity for breach verification.
3 / 5

Evaluation Rules

  • Errors (Rejected): Username inclusion, empty input, >300 breaches.
  • Warnings (Interactive): 1–300 breaches, <4 numbers & special characters combined (at least 1 special char mandatory), missing uppercase, birth year (1950–2026).
4 / 5

k-Anonymity Privacy

  • Local SHA-1 hashing of password.
  • Only first 5 characters sent to HaveIBeenPwned API.
  • Remaining 35 characters matched locally on device.
